The flood peak on the Beijiang River in Guangdong Province passed through Qingyuan city by 23:00 hours last night, and the water level was recorded as 14.72 m, about 4.22 m above the danger mark; the speed was 17,400 cu m per second.
The flood has caused serious losses to people along both banks of the river, especially in Changsha, according to the sources.
The flood peak passed Changsha on 19 June, but the water level at the Changsha Station was still at 25 meters at 0200 today [1800 GMT 20 June].
